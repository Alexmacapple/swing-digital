Charlotte Henschel

Charlotte Henschel
A life to paint

Mixed reality/theatre

A suitcase left forgotten for forty years, under a bed.

When it opens, the story of Charlotte Henschel unfolds. A life spent painting despite war, exile, camps and anonymity.

In mixed reality, the public crosses its century.

A journey inhabited by this irrepressible need to create, against all odds.

Charlotte Henschel
A life to paint

Charlotte painted
more than 220 paintings
until the age of 90.

Her work appears in private collections, as well as at the MAHJ (Paris) and the Henri-Martin museum (Cahors).
