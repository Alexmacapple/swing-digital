Augmented spaces

What is an augmented space?

An augmented space is a physical place transformed into a narrative space through mixed reality, sound, image, acting and interaction. At Swing Digital, in partnership with Pixihead Studios, this format serves narratives where the audience walks, observes and moves through the story rather than watching it from a distance.

Augmented spaces Swing Digital/Pixihead, the essential
Formats Mixed reality, immersive theatre, interactive installations and transmedia stories.
Places Museums, galleries, public places, heritage spaces and staged environments.
Role of the audience The audience wanders, interacts with the space and follows a dramaturgy anchored in the place.
Key considerations Reservation terms, minimum age and accessibility depend on each experience.
