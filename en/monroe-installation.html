Transcript - The installation at the Galerie Joseph

The labyrinth

So like that, you are in an August blues, and make the most of it, you have the right to play with the great lady of the poster, the Monroe experience. It's a magical labyrinth in the hidden rooms of the Joseph Paris 3rd gallery.

March 1954

When it's already snack time and everything is closed, it's the opportunity to mature your most intimate thoughts by immersing yourself in her unfinished novel. Several entry points are offered to you and everyone follows their own path. It is March 1954, Marilyn returns from Korea where she boosted the morale of the G.I.

Solar fighter Marilyn, she faces everything that is supposed to destroy her, she addresses all those who are waiting for their life to begin, those who are looking for a place in this world.

The shutters of the route

The Monroe experience is explored through photographs by Milton Greene, a graphic novel, an installation, a work of virtual reality, a play also Marilyn Monroe unfinished confession, and a space dedicated to the soundscape of Michel Berthier composed for the occasion. It's at the Joseph gallery, go dressed in your white ruffled dress to the ventilation grill where you will be in the shoes of Marilyn.
